NGS AI Assistant

Get suggestions based on your own skill gaps, certifications, goals and training. Suggestions are for you and your manager to review. They are not decisions.

About this AI feature
  • Purpose: helps you pick training that closes the gaps between your skills and your job role's requirements.
  • Data sent: your job role title, skill levels, certification status, goals, assigned training and the NGS training catalog. Your name, email, ID number and contact details are never sent.
  • What it produces: a short list of suggested next steps with a reason for each.
  • How it is used: as a starting point for a conversation with your manager. Nothing is changed or assigned automatically.
  • Limitations: it can be wrong or out of date, it only knows what is in the portal, and it only recommends courses in the NGS catalog. Always check with your manager.
